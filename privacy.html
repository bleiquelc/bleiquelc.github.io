PRIVACY

INFINITO has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies.

Microphone (optional). Your browser asks for it, if it has no answer yet, when you first pinch Begin, or if you tap “Let INFINITO hear your breath — nothing is recorded or sent” on the page, or “Let INFINITO hear your breath” on the card that follows Begin. INFINITO then measures, in memory, how loud your breath is. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent. If you said no and want the microphone back, allow it in this site’s permissions in your browser, then reload.

Hands, head and room. Hand tracking, head position and room data (planes and mesh from WebXR) are used on your device during the session. They are not stored or sent.

On your device. Your browser’s local storage keeps small items: whether you have visited, the visitors you have seen, your garden and the snail that lives in it, and a harp motif. Session storage keeps your “Again” or “Begin again as a new visitor” choice for one reload. None of it leaves your device. Clear this site’s data in your browser to remove it.

After loading. The page’s content policy lets it fetch only its own files, so it is set not to send data to any other site.

Hosting. The page is served by a static web host. Like most hosts, it may log technical data, such as your IP address, under its own policy. INFINITO runs no analytics and holds no personal data.

Contact. For questions about this page, use the INFINITO entry on Devpost (Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026).
